## 1. Executive Strategy & South African Market Context

### The Core Problem in South African Secondary Education
1. **The 45-Minute In-Class Reality:** Teachers have 45 minutes to manage 35–45 students, cover administrative work, and deliver complex CAPS syllabus material (e.g., Euclidean Geometry, Financial Maths, Partnership Ledgers). In-class time is statistically insufficient for mastery.
2. **The "Tutor Tax":** Private tutoring in South Africa costs **R250 to R350+ per single hour**. For a student taking Maths and Physical Sciences twice a week, parents spend upwards of **R2,800/month**—an impossible financial burden for most middle-class households.
3. **Data Inequality & Load Shedding:** Video-heavy learning platforms (YouTube, recorded webinars, Zoom tutoring) consume **450MB to 800MB per hour**. In South Africa, where mobile data is expensive and Stage 4 load shedding knocks out cell towers and home fibre, video-based tutoring collapses.
4. **The Method Mark Blindspot:** Learners lose distinctions not because they don't know the theory, but because they miss **Method Marks `[M]`** and **Consequential Accuracy Marks `[CA]`** in step-by-step working. Traditional online tools only check the final answer ("Right" or "Wrong"), inducing false failure anxiety.

### Fundile's Competitive Moat & Value Proposition
- **Deterministic Cognitive Engine:** 259+ CAPS topics with infinite variable generation for numerical subjects (Maths, Technical Maths, Maths Lit, Accounting, Physical Sciences).
- **Stepwise Working Tracker:** Line-by-line working pad that marks like an official Senior National Certificate (NSC) examiner, isolating the exact line an error occurred and preserving carry-over accuracy.
- **Ultra-Lightweight PWA (< 2MB WebAPK):** Instant install, zero buffering, runs with minimal battery drain during load shedding, and operates on virtually zero data.
- **Radical Pricing Accessibility:**
  - **Monthly Pass:** **R149 / month** (flexible, cancel anytime — less than a movie ticket or 25 minutes with a tutor).
  - **School Term Pass (3 Months):** **R349 / term** (~R116/mo, save R98 — *Most Popular*).
  - **Annual Distinction Pass (12 Months):** **R999 / year** (~R83/mo, save R789 — *Ultimate Matric Insurance*).
  - **Trial:** 2-week risk-free trial at `fundile.co.za`.
