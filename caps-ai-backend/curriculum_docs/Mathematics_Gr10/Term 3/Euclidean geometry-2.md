# Euclidean geometry-2



\-Geometry (from the Greek “geo” = earth and “metria” = measure)arose as the field of knowledge dealing with spatial relationships. 

\-Geometry can be split into Euclidean geometry and analytical geometry. 

\-Analytical geometry deals with space and shape using algebra and a coordinate system. 

\-Euclidean geometry deals with space and shape using a system of logical deductions.

### Example

In parallelogram ABCD, the bisectors of the angles (AW , BX, CY and DZ) have been constructed. You are also given  AB = CD, AD = BC, AB ∥ CD, AD ∥ BC, Aˆ = Cˆ, Bˆ = Dˆ. Prove that MNOP is a parallelogram.



DIAGRAM\[

#### SOLUTION

##### Step1: 

Use properties of the parallelogram to fill in on the diagram all equal sides and angles.



##### Step 2: Prove that Angle Mˆ\_2 = angle Oˆ\_2

In triangle CDZ and triangle ABX,



DCˆZ = BAˆX (given)

Dˆ\_1 =Bˆ\_1 (given)

DC = AB (given)

∴ △CDZ ≡ △ABX (AAS)

∴ CZ = AX

and angle CZˆD = AXˆB



In triangle XAM and triangle ZCO,



XAˆM = ZCˆO (given: △CDZ ≡ △ABX)

AXˆM = CZˆO (proved above)

AX = CZ (proved above)

∴ △XAM ≡ △ZCO (AAS)

∴ Mˆ\_1 = Oˆ\_1

but Mˆ\_1 = Mˆ\_2 (vertically opposite ∠s are =) 

and Oˆ\_1 = Oˆ\_2 (vertically opposite ∠s are =)

∴ Mˆ\_2 = Oˆ\_2



##### Step 3: Similarly, we can show that Nˆ\_2 = Pˆ\_2

First show △ADW ≡ △CBY. Then show △PDW ≡ △NBY.



##### Step 4: Conclusion

Both pairs of opposite angles of are equal. Therefore is a   parallelogram.
