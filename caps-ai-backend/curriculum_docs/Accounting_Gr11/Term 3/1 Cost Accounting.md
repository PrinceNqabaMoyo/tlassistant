## NOTES

### Costs of production

#### Manufacturing costs/production costs

##### Direct/Prime costs

###### Raw materials/ Direct materials (issued or used)

* Any materials used in the final product
* Processed or unprocessed materials
* These materials are clearly visible in the final product



###### Direct labour

* Refers to workers directly involved in the production process, workers 'touching' the product in production or 'working' on it.
* Includes all costs to company (wages or salaries plus employer's contribution for pension fund, UIF or medical aid)



##### Factory overheads

###### Indirect materials/ Consumables (issued or used)

* Materials used in the production process but relatively insignificant



###### Indirect labour

* Includes salaries or wages of factory manager, factory supervisor, security guards for factory, workers handling raw materials
* Employer's contribution for pension fund, UIF or medical aid must be included



###### Depreciation

\-Only for factory equipment or machinery or vehicles only used in factory.



###### Maintenance and repairs

\-Of factory equipment or machinery or vehicles only used in factory



###### Insurance

\-On factory plant and equipment or machinery or vehicles only used in factory



###### Rent

Of factory plant or equipment



###### Water and electricity

Used in factory



###### Other

Any cost directly related to factory or equipment and machinery used in factory.



##### Variable costs

\-Costs directly related to the level of production. They increase if more products are produced.

* Raw materials/Direct materials (issued or used)
* Direct labour
* Selling and distribution costs





##### Fixed costs

\-Costs that must be paid irrespective of the levels of production. Must be paid even if there is no production

* Factory overheads
* Administrative costs





#### Non-manufacturing costs

##### Administrative costs

###### All costs related to the office

* Rent and insurance of office buildings or equipment used in office
* Depreciation on office equipment
* Water and electricity use in office
* Salaries and wages of office staff, receptionist, office cleaners (including employer's contribution for UIF, pension fund or medical aid)
* Maintenance of office buildings and equipment
* Telephone costs
* Water and electricity used in office



##### Selling and distribution costs (Marketing)

###### All costs related to shop or agents handling sales

* Commission paid to agents
* Depreciation paid on shop equipment
* Depreciation on vehicles used by agents
* Rent and insurance of shop or shop equipment
* Telephone bills of agents
* Water and electricity used in shop
* Bad debts
