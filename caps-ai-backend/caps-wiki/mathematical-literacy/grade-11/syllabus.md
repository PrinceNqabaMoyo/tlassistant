### 4. CONTENT & COGNITIVE SCOPE
The curriculum emphasizes the application of mathematical concepts to real-world scenarios. Assessment tasks are categorized into:
*   **Assignments:** Focused on the application of routine and complex procedures to specific contexts (e.g., household budgets, electricity costs).
*   **Investigations:** Focused on higher-order cognitive skills, requiring students to identify relationships, compare systems, and analyze data trends (e.g., comparing interest rates or cell phone tariffs).

*Note: While specific cognitive level weightings are defined in the broader CAPS policy document, these tasks specifically target the "Complex Procedures" and "Problem Solving" levels of the cognitive hierarchy.*
