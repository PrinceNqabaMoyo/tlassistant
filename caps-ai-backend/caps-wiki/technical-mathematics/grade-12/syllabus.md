### CONTENT & COGNITIVE SCOPE

#### 1. Complex Numbers
*   **Core Concepts:** Definition of the complex number system $\mathbb{C}$, the imaginary unit $i$, conjugates, Argand representation, polar (trigonometric) form, and algebraic manipulation.
*   **Skills:** 
    *   Simplification of expressions involving square roots of negative numbers.
    *   Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division of complex numbers.
    *   Solving systems of equations involving complex variables.
*   **Cognitive Levels:** 
    *   **Knowledge (K):** Recall of definitions and basic properties.
    *   **Routine Procedures (R):** Standard algebraic simplification and solving linear equations with complex coefficients.

#### 2. Polynomials
*   **Core Concepts:** Third-degree polynomials, Remainder Theorem, Factor Theorem, functional notation.
*   **Skills:** 
    *   Factorisation of cubic polynomials.
    *   Application of the Factor Theorem to find roots.
    *   Solving cubic equations.
*   **Cognitive Levels:**
    *   **Routine Procedures (R):** Application of standard factorisation algorithms and theorem-based solving.

### 2. CONTENT & COGNITIVE SCOPE

**Core Concepts:**
*   **Limits:** Intuitive grasp of limits and the transition from secant to tangent.
*   **Differentiation:** First principles and the power rule.
*   **Applications:** Tangent equations, cubic function sketching, optimisation, and kinematics.

**Cognitive Levels & Depth:**
*   **Knowledge (K):** Recall of derivative notation ($D_x, \frac{d}{dx}, f'(x)$).
*   **Routine Procedures (R):** Applying the power rule; finding derivatives at specific points (e.g., $x = -1$).
*   **Complex Procedures (C):** Sketching cubic graphs ($y = -x^3 + 4x^2 - x$) by finding intercepts and stationary points.
*   **Problem Solving (P):** Optimisation problems and practical applications in technical fields (e.g., displacement $s = u + \frac{1}{2}at^2$).

### Content & Cognitive Scope

#### 1. Integration
*   **Core Concepts:** Definite vs. Indefinite integrals, Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (implied), Area under a curve.
*   **Skills:**
    *   Evaluating definite integrals: $\int_{0}^{1} x \, dx$ (K)
    *   Evaluating definite integrals of polynomials: $\int_{1}^{2} (x^3 + 2x^2 - 3) \, dx$ (R)
    *   Calculating area bounded by $y = f(x)$ and the $x$-axis (C).
*   **Depth of Treatment:** Focus on standard forms and basic polynomial integration.

#### 2. Analytical Geometry
*   **Core Concepts:** Circle properties, Tangents to circles, Intersection of loci, Ellipse standard form.
*   **Skills:**
    *   Deriving circle equations from points $(x, y)$ and origin $(0, 0)$ (R).
    *   Calculating tangent equations at specific points (R).
    *   Solving systems of equations for intersection points (R).
    *   Visualizing and plotting the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$.
*   **Depth of Treatment:** Restricted to circles with centres at the origin $(0, 0)$.

### 4. Mathematical Context (Trigonometry Example)
The curriculum requires the application of trigonometric principles to solve for heights in 3D space. Given a tower $TP$ on a horizontal plane with points $Q$ and $R$:
*   Angle of elevation from $Q$ to $T = 20^\circ$
*   $P\hat{Q}R = 150^\circ$
*   $Q\hat{P}R = 10^\circ$
*   $PR = 28\text{m}$

To find the height $TP$, students must utilize the relationship:
$$\tan(20^\circ) = \frac{TP}{PQ}$$
Which requires first determining the length of $PQ$ using the Sine Rule in $\triangle PQR$:
$$\frac{PQ}{\sin(R)} = \frac{PR}{\sin(Q)}$$
