### SECTION 2: Business Studies Curriculum Specifications

#### 2.1 Subject Overview
Business Studies focuses on the knowledge, skills, attitudes, and values necessary for informed, productive, ethical, and responsible participation in both formal and informal economic sectors. The curriculum integrates business principles, theory, and practice to foster entrepreneurial initiatives, sustainable enterprise development, and economic growth.

#### 2.2 Curriculum Weighting Matrix
The curriculum is structured into four main pillars, each carrying an equal weighting of 25% of the total curriculum scope.

| Pillar | Weighting | Topics |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Business Environment** | 25% | 1. Micro, market and macro environments<br>2. Business sectors<br>3. Contemporary socioeconomic issues |
| **Business Venture** | 25% | 4. Entrepreneurship<br>5. Business opportunity and related factors<br>6. Business Plan<br>7. Management and Leadership<br>8. Forms of ownership<br>9. Setting up a business<br>10. Contracts<br>11. Business location<br>12. Investment: securities and insurance<br>13. Presentation of business information |
| **Business Role** | 25% | 14. Creative thinking and problem-solving<br>15. Self-management, professionalism and ethics<br>16. Human rights, inclusivity and environmental issues<br>17. Social Responsibility<br>18. Stress, crisis, change and conflict management<br>19. Relationship and team performance |
| **Business Operation** | 25% | 20. Business functions<br>21. Quality of performance |

#### 2.3 Educational Objectives
Business Studies is designed to ensure that learners:
*   Acquire and apply essential business knowledge, skills, and principles to conduct business productively and profitably within dynamic environments.
*   Create business opportunities, employ creative problem-solving techniques, manage risks, and uphold rights and environmental sustainability.
*   Apply fundamental leadership and management principles to collaborate effectively toward the accomplishment of business goals.

#### 4. Mathematical & Financial Contexts
When conducting the "Financial analysis" for the Business Plan (Grade 10), learners must apply basic financial literacy. While specific formulas are context-dependent, the following standard notation applies to business viability calculations:

*   **Net Profit:** $P = R - C$ (where $R$ is Revenue and $C$ is Total Costs).
*   **Break-even Point:** $Q = \frac{FC}{P - V}$ (where $Q$ is quantity, $FC$ is Fixed Costs, $P$ is Price per unit, and $V$ is Variable cost per unit).

## 2. Content & Cognitive Scope

### Core Concepts & Depth of Treatment

#### Topic 1: Influences on Business Environments
*   **Control Factors:** Analysis of the degree of control over micro-environment (high), market environment (low), and macro-environment (very low).
*   **Micro-environment:** Focus on mission, objectives, management structure, resources, and organizational culture.
*   **Market Environment:** Focus on customers, suppliers, competitors, regulators, and strategic allies/unions.
*   **Macro-environment:** Focus on economic, technological, and political environments.

#### Topic 2: Challenges of the Business Environment
*   **Micro-environment Challenges:** Difficult employees, lack of vision/mission, inadequate management skills, unions, strikes, go-slows.
*   **Market Environment Challenges:** Competition, supply shortages, changes in consumer behavior, demographics, psychographics, socio-cultural factors.
*   **Macro-environment Challenges:** Income level fluctuations, political changes, legal legislation, labor restrictions, micro-lending, globalization, international challenges, social values, socio-economic issues.

#### Topic 3: Adapting to Challenges
*   **Strategic Responses:** Information management, mergers, takeovers, acquisitions, alliances, organizational design/flexibility, social responsibility.
*   **Lobbying, Networking, and Power Relations:**
    *   **Lobbying:** Hedging against inflation, bargaining sessions.
    *   **Networking:** Identifying new customers.
    *   **Power Relations:** Strategic alliances.

### Cognitive Levels
*   **Knowledge:** Identification of environmental factors and specific challenges.
*   **Routine Procedures:** Categorizing challenges into the correct business environment (micro, market, or macro).
*   **Complex Procedures:** Analyzing the relationship between business adaptation strategies and specific environmental challenges.
*   **Problem Solving:** Evaluating the effectiveness of lobbying and strategic responses in mitigating macro-environmental risks.

# SECTION 4: ASSESSMENT SPECIFICATIONS

## 1. Definition and Purpose of Assessment
Assessment is defined as a process that measures individual learners' attainment of knowledge (content, concepts, and skills). The data and information obtained are used to:
*   Inform learners about their strengths, weaknesses, and progress.
*   Assist teachers, parents, and stakeholders in making decisions regarding the learning process and learner progress.

Assessment must be mapped directly against the subject content and intended learning objectives.

## 2. Informal (Daily) Assessment
*   **Nature:** Planned teaching and learning activities integrated into every lesson.
*   **Methods:** Question and answer sessions, classwork, short written tasks, open-book tests, or homework exercises.
*   **Purpose:** 
    *   To provide immediate feedback to enhance learning and teaching.
    *   To scaffold the acquisition of knowledge and skills.
    *   To serve as a stepping stone to formal assessment tasks.
*   **Learner Involvement:** Includes self-assessment and peer assessment to encourage reflection on performance.
*   **Recording:** Not formally recorded for promotion or certification purposes (unless the teacher chooses to use a simple checklist for feedback).

## 3. Formal Assessment
*   **Nature:** A systematic method of evaluating learner progress within a grade and subject.
*   **Examples:** Projects, oral presentations, demonstrations, performances, tests, examinations, and practical tasks.
*   **Structure:** Formal assessment tasks constitute a year-long **Programme of Assessment (PoA)**.
*   **Weighting:** These tasks are school-based and weighted according to specific grade requirements.
