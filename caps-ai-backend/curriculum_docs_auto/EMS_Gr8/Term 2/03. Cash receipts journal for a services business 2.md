# Financial Literacy: Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ)

## Overview of Topics
In this topic, learners will look at:
* Entering of cash transactions in the Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ)
* Balancing of the CRJ
* The effect of cash transactions on the accounting equation

## Teaching Guidelines for Accounts
Accounts 1 and 2 do not need to be in the order above. If learners are advanced, the column heading of Account 1 can be changed to **Account debited** and the column heading of Account 2 to **Account credited**. 

* Ensure that learners understand where they must insert the names of the accounts in the table.
* Explain that they must insert the name of a column in the CRJ in the column headed **Account 1** (such as *Bank* or *Current income*), and the name of the Sundry account that would appear in the *Details* column of the Sundry accounts of the CRJ in the column headed **Account 2** (such as *Capital* or *Rent income*). Learners should already be familiar with these account names from previous examples of accounting concepts in Topic 5.

## Consolidation & Revision Questions

### 1. Source Documents
* **Name the three source documents used in the CRJ:** 
  * Receipt
  * Cash register roll
  * Deposit slip
* **Name the source document that is used to deposit the total receipts at the end of a day:** 
  * Deposit slip

### 2. CRJ Heading Requirements
* **Name three items that must appear in the heading of a CRJ:**
  1. Name of the business
  2. Month in which the transactions take place
  3. Number of the journal

### 3. Cross-Checking the CRJ
* **Explain how you cross-check a CRJ:** 
  By adding together the totals of all the columns, including the Sundry accounts column. This total must equal the total of the Bank column.

### 4. Rules for Completing a CRJ
* **Name two important rules to remember when completing a CRJ:**
  1. Every transaction must appear in the **Analysis of receipts** column and in one other column.
  2. Each amount is deposited daily. If there is more than one transaction on the same day, the amounts are added together in the **Bank** column as one amount.

### 5. Direct Deposits
* **What does a 'direct deposit' mean? Explain how it is entered in the CRJ:** 
  A 'direct deposit' usually takes place when a customer or the owner deposits a large amount of money directly into the business’s bank account. This transaction is entered directly into the **Bank** column and does *not* appear in the **Analysis of receipts** column.
