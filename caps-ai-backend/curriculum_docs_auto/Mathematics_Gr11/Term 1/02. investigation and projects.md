## Key Concepts

### 1. Simple Interest
Simple interest is calculated only on the initial principal amount. The formula is generally expressed as:
$$A = P(1 + i \times n)$$
Where:
*   $A$ = Accumulated amount
*   $P$ = Principal amount
*   $i$ = Interest rate (as a decimal)
*   $n$ = Number of time periods

### 2. Compound Interest
Compound interest is calculated on the principal amount plus any interest accumulated from previous periods. The formula is:
$$A = P(1 + i)^n$$

### 3. Foreign Exchange Rates
This section deals with the conversion of currency values based on current market exchange rates, expressed as:
$$\text{Amount in Foreign Currency} = \text{Amount in Local Currency} \times \text{Exchange Rate}$$

### Simple Interest Applications

The simple interest formula is defined as:
$$A = P(1 + in)$$

Where:
*   $A$ = Accumulated amount (final value)
*   $P$ = Principal amount (initial investment)
*   $i$ = Interest rate (as a decimal)
*   $n$ = Number of years

---

#### Worked Example 6: Calculating Principal ($P$)
**Problem:** A bank offers a savings account with $6\%$ p.a. simple interest. How much must be invested now to accumulate R 15 000 in 5 years?

**Solution:**
1. Identify variables:
   *   $A = 15\,000$
   *   $i = \frac{6}{100} = 0,06$
   *   $n = 5$
   *   $P = ?$

2. Apply formula:
   $$15\,000 = P(1 + (0,06 \times 5))$$
   $$15\,000 = P(1,3)$$
   $$P = \frac{15\,000}{1,3}$$
   $$P = 11\,538,46$$

**Result:** You should invest **R 11 538,46**.

---

#### Worked Example 7: Calculating Time ($n$)
**Problem:** Sally wants to invest R 1 000 to accumulate R 2 500 at a simple interest rate of $8,2\%$ p.a. How many years will it take?

**Solution:**
1. Identify variables:
   *   $A = 2\,500$
   *   $P = 1\,000$
   *   $i = 0,082$
   *   $n = ?$

2. Apply formula:
   $$2\,500 = 1\,000(1 + 0,082n)$$
   $$\frac{2\,500}{1\,000} = 1 + 0,082n$$
   $$\frac{2\,500}{1\,000} - 1 = 0,082n$$
   $$n = \left( \frac{2\,500}{1\,000} - 1 \right) \div 0,082$$
   $$n = 18,3$$

**Result:** It will take **19 years** for the money to grow to R 2 500.

---

#### Worked Example 8: Calculating Interest Rate ($i$)
**Problem:** Joseph deposited R 5 000. When his son turned 21 (from age 5), the balance was R 18 000. Calculate the simple interest rate.

**Solution:**
1. Identify variables:
   *   $A = 18\,000$
   *   $P = 5\,000$
   *   $n = 21 - 5 = 16$ years
   *   $i = ?$

2. Apply formula:
   $$18\,000 = 5\,000(1 + i \times 16)$$
   $$\frac{18\,000}{5\,000} = 1 + 16i$$
   $$\frac{18\,000}{5\,000} - 1 = 16i$$
   $$i = \left( \frac{18\,000}{5\,000} - 1 \right) \div 16$$
   $$i = 0,1625$$

**Result:** The interest rate was **$16,25\%$**.

### Simple Interest: Calculating the Interest Rate

When dealing with simple interest problems where the final amount ($A$), the principal amount ($P$), and the time period ($n$) are known, you can calculate the interest rate ($i$) by rearranging the simple interest formula.

#### The Formula
The standard simple interest formula is:
$$A = P(1 + in)$$

To solve for the interest rate ($i$), we rearrange the formula as follows:
1. Divide by $P$: $\frac{A}{P} = 1 + in$
2. Subtract 1: $\frac{A}{P} - 1 = in$
3. Divide by $n$: $i = \frac{\frac{A}{P} - 1}{n}$

---

#### Worked Example 9
**Problem:** Methuli deposited R 6 610. When his son was 18 (12 years later), the value was R 11 131,24. Find the interest rate.

**Given:**
*   $A = R 11 131,24$
*   $P = R 6 610$
*   $n = 18 - 6 = 12$ years

**Calculation:**
$$i = \frac{\left( \frac{11 131,24}{6 610} \right) - 1}{12}$$
$$i = 0,057$$
**Answer:** The interest rate is **5,7% per annum**.

---

#### Worked Example 10
**Problem:** Phillip deposited R 5 040. When his son was 18 (12 years later), the value was R 7 338,24. Find the interest rate.

**Given:**
*   $A = R 7 338,24$
*   $P = R 5 040$
*   $n = 18 - 6 = 12$ years

**Calculation:**
$$i = \frac{\left( \frac{7 338,24}{5 040} \right) - 1}{12}$$
$$i = 0,038$$
**Answer:** The interest rate is **3,8% per annum**.

---

#### Worked Example 11
**Problem:** Lefu deposited R 2 580. When his son was 20 (10 years later), the value was R 3 689,40. Find the interest rate.

**Given:**
*   $A = R 3 689,40$
*   $P = R 2 580$
*   $n = 20 - 10 = 10$ years

**Calculation:**
To find the rate, apply the rearranged formula:
$$i = \frac{\left( \frac{3 689,40}{2 580} \right) - 1}{10}$$

### Calculating Simple Interest Variables

When an investment grows at a simple interest rate, we use the standard simple interest formula:
$$A = P(1 + in)$$

Where:
*   $A$ = Accumulated amount
*   $P$ = Principal amount
*   $i$ = Interest rate (as a decimal)
*   $n$ = Number of years

---

#### Worked Example: Calculating the Interest Rate ($i$)
To find the interest rate, rearrange the formula to make $i$ the subject:

1.  Start with: $A = P(1 + in)$
2.  Divide by $P$: $\frac{A}{P} = 1 + in$
3.  Subtract 1: $\frac{A}{P} - 1 = in$
4.  Divide by $n$: $i = \frac{\frac{A}{P} - 1}{n}$

**Calculation:**
$$i = \frac{(\frac{3689,40}{2580}) - 1}{10}$$
$$i = 0,043$$
$$i = 4,3\% \text{ per annum}$$

### Calculating the Number of Years ($n$) for Simple Interest

To determine the time period required to reach a specific future value using simple interest, we rearrange the simple interest formula $A = P(1 + in)$ to make $n$ the subject:

$$A = P(1 + in)$$
$$\frac{A}{P} = 1 + in$$
$$\frac{A}{P} - 1 = in$$
$$n = \frac{\frac{A}{P} - 1}{i}$$

**Worked Example:**
Given $A = 14\,448$, $P = 3010$, and $i = 0,119$:

$$n = \frac{(\frac{14\,448}{3010}) - 1}{0,119}$$
$$n = 31,9$$

Since we must reach the goal, we round up to the nearest integer:
$$n = 32 \text{ years}$$

## 9.3 Compound Interest

### Calculating the Interest Rate ($i$) in Compound Interest

To determine the interest rate when the principal ($P$), accumulated amount ($A$), and time period ($n$) are known, we rearrange the compound interest formula $A = P(1 + i)^n$ to make $i$ the subject:

1. Start with the formula: $A = P(1 + i)^n$
2. Divide by $P$: $\frac{A}{P} = (1 + i)^n$
3. Take the $n^{th}$ root of both sides: $(\frac{A}{P})^{\frac{1}{n}} = 1 + i$
4. Subtract 1: $i = (\frac{A}{P})^{\frac{1}{n}} - 1$

---

### Worked Example: Bongani's Investment

**Problem:** Bongani invests R 6 110 into an account. At the end of 7 years, he receives a lump sum of R 6 904,30. Calculate the compound interest rate offered by the bank (correct to one decimal place).

**Solution:**

**1. Identify the given information:**
*   $A = R\, 6\, 904,30$
*   $P = R\, 6\, 110$
*   $n = 7$
*   $i = ?$

**2. Apply the formula:**
$$i = \left(\frac{A}{P}\right)^{\frac{1}{n}} - 1$$

**3. Substitute and calculate:**
$$i = \left(\frac{6904,30}{6110}\right)^{\frac{1}{7}} - 1$$
$$i = 0,018$$

**4. Final Answer:**
The interest rate is $1,8\%$ per annum.

### Hire Purchase Calculations

#### Worked Example 1: Microwave Purchase
**Scenario:** A microwave has a cash price of R 4 400. A deposit of 10% is required, with the remaining balance paid off over 1 year at an interest rate of 9% p.a.

**a) What is the principal loan amount?**
To find the principal loan amount ($P$), first calculate the deposit, then subtract it from the cash price.

1. Calculate the deposit:
   $$\text{deposit} = 4400 \times \frac{10}{100} = 440$$

2. Calculate the principal loan amount:
   $$P = \text{cash price} - \text{deposit}$$
   $$P = 4400 - 440 = \text{R } 3960,00$$

**b) What is the accumulated loan amount?**
Use the simple interest formula to find the total amount ($A$) to be repaid.
*   $P = \text{R } 3960,00$
*   $i = \frac{9}{100} = 0,09$
*   $n = 1$

$$A = P(1 + in)$$
$$A = 3960,00(1 + 0,09 \times 1)$$
$$A = \text{R } 4316,40$$

**c) What are Angelique's monthly repayments?**
Divide the accumulated amount ($A$) by the total number of months (12).

$$\text{Monthly repayment} = \frac{A}{\text{no. of months}}$$
$$\text{Monthly repayment} = \frac{4316,40}{12} = \text{R } 359,70$$

**d) What is the total amount she has paid for the microwave?**
Add the accumulated loan amount to the initial deposit.

$$\text{Total amount} = A + \text{deposit amount}$$
$$\text{Total amount} = 4316,40 + 440 = \text{R } 4756,40$$

*   $n$ = Number of years

### Financial Mathematics: Inflation and Compound Interest

When calculating the effects of inflation over time, we use the compound interest formula:
$$A = P(1 + i)^n$$

Where:
*   $A$ = Future value (current price)
*   $P$ = Present value (original price or value in the past)
*   $i$ = Interest rate (inflation rate as a decimal)
*   $n$ = Number of years

To find the value in the past ($P$), we rearrange the formula:
$$P = \frac{A}{(1 + i)^n}$$

---

#### Worked Example 4: Calculating Past Price
**Question:** A box of raisins costs R 24 today. How much did it cost 4 years ago if the average rate of inflation was 13% p.a.?

**Given:**
*   $A = 24$
*   $i = \frac{13}{100} = 0,13$
*   $n = 4$

**Calculation:**
$$P = \frac{24}{(1 + 0,13)^4}$$
$$P = \frac{24}{(1,13)^4}$$
$$P \approx R 14,72$$

### Simple Interest Applications

The simple interest formula is defined as:
$$A = P(1 + in)$$

Where:
*   $A$ = Accumulated amount (Final value)
*   $P$ = Principal amount (Initial investment)
*   $i$ = Interest rate (as a decimal)
*   $n$ = Number of time periods (years)

---

#### Worked Example 2: Calculating Accumulated Amount
**Problem:** An amount of R 1 740 is invested in a savings account which pays simple interest at a rate of 7% p.a. Calculate the balance accumulated by the end of 6 years.

**Solution:**
1. Identify variables:
   *   $P = 1740$
   *   $i = \frac{7}{100} = 0,07$
   *   $n = 6$
2. Apply formula:
   $$A = P(1 + in)$$
   $$A = 1740(1 + (0,07 \times 6))$$
   $$A = R 2470,80$$

#### Worked Example 3: Calculating Principal Amount
**Problem:** Adam opens a savings account when he is 13. He would like to have R 50 000 by the time he is 18. If the savings account offers simple interest at a rate of 8,5% per annum, how much money should he invest now to reach his goal?

   *   $A = 50 000$
   *   $i = \frac{8,5}{100} = 0,085$
   *   $n = 18 - 13 = 5$
2. Apply formula:
   $$A = P(1 + in)$$
   $$50 000 = P(1 + (0,085 \times 5))$$

#### Worked Example 4: Calculating Interest Rate
**Problem:** When his son was 4 years old, Dumile made a deposit of R 6 700 in the bank. The investment grew at a simple interest rate and when Dumile’s son was 24 years old, the value of the investment was R 11 524. At what rate was the money invested?

**Solution:**

   *   $A = 11 524$

#### Worked Example 5: Calculating the Interest Rate
**Problem:** Jared deposited R 5 850. When his son turned 35 (from age 7), the investment grew to R 11 746,80. Calculate the interest rate.

**Given:**
*   $A = 11\ 746,80$
*   $P = 5\ 850$

### Calculating Time ($n$) in Simple Interest
To find the number of years ($n$) required to reach a specific future value, we rearrange the simple interest formula $A = P(1 + in)$:

1. Divide by $P$: $\frac{A}{P} = 1 + in$
2. Subtract 1: $\frac{A}{P} - 1 = in$
3. Divide by $i$: $n = \frac{\frac{A}{P} - 1}{i}$

**Worked Example:**
Given $A = 16\,620$, $P = 5\,540$, and $i = 0,091$:
$$n = \frac{\left(\frac{16\,620}{5\,540}\right) - 1}{0,091}$$
$$n = 21,9780...$$
Rounding up to the nearest integer, $n = 22$ years.

### Compound Interest: Calculating Principal ($P$)

When the future accumulated amount ($A$) is known, and you need to determine the initial investment (the principal, $P$), you rearrange the compound interest formula:

$$A = P(1 + i)^n$$

To solve for $P$, divide both sides by $(1 + i)^n$:

$$P = \frac{A}{(1 + i)^n}$$

---

*   $P = ?$

$$P = \frac{A}{(1 + i)^n}$$
$$P = \frac{61\,500}{(1 + 0,082)^4}$$
$$P = R\,44\,871,03$$

### Compound Interest Calculations

The formula for calculating the accumulated amount ($A$) using compound interest is:
$$A = P(1 + i)^n$$

Where:
*   $P$ = Principal amount (initial investment or loan)
*   $i$ = Interest rate (as a decimal)
*   $n$ = Number of time periods

---

#### Worked Example: Investment of R 1500 for 3 years at 6% p.a.

**Given:**
*   $P = 1500$
*   $i = 0,06$
*   $n = 3$
*   $A = ?$

**Calculation:**
$$A = P(1 + i)^n$$
$$A = 1500(1 + 0,06)^3$$
$$A = R\,1786,52$$

**Interest Earned:**
$$\text{Interest} = A - P$$
$$\text{Interest} = 1786,52 - 1500 = R\,286,52$$

---

#### Worked Example: Loan of R 800 for 1 year at 16% p.a.

**Given:**
*   $P = 800$
*   $i = 0,16$
*   $n = 1$
*   $A = ?$

**Calculation:**
$$A = P(1 + i)^n$$
$$A = 800(1 + 0,16)^1$$
$$A = R\,928$$

**Interest Charged:**
$$\text{Interest} = A - P$$
$$\text{Interest} = 928 - 800 = R\,128$$

### Compound Interest: Calculating the Interest Rate ($i$)

To find the interest rate when the principal ($P$), accumulated amount ($A$), and time period ($n$) are known, use the compound interest formula:
$$A = P(1 + i)^n$$

#### Worked Example: Calculating Interest Rate
**Problem:** Christopher invests R 4480 into an account which pays out a lump sum at the end of 7 years. If he gets R 6496,00 at the end of the period, what compound interest rate did the bank offer him? (Give your answer correct to one decimal place).

**Solution:**
1. **Identify the given information:**
   * $A = 6496$
   * $P = 4480$
   * $n = 7$
   * $i = ?$

2. **Substitute into the formula:**
   $$A = P(1 + i)^n$$
   $$6496 = 4480(1 + i)^7$$

3. **Solve for $i$:**
   Divide both sides by 4480:
   $$\frac{6496}{4480} = (1 + i)^7$$
   $$1,45 = (1 + i)^7$$

   Take the 7th root of both sides:
   $$\sqrt[7]{1,45} = 1 + i$$
   $$1,0545... = 1 + i$$
   $$i = 1,0545... - 1$$
   $$i = 0,0545...$$

4. **Final Answer:**
   $$i \approx 5,5\% \text{ per annum}$$

#### Problem 35: Xolile's CD Player Purchase
Xolile wants to buy a CD player that costs $£ 140$. The current exchange rate is $£ 1 = R 14$. She estimates that the exchange rate will drop to $R 10$ in a month.

**a) How much will the CD player cost in rands, if she buys it now?**
*   **Solution:**
    $$\text{Cost} = 140 \times R 14$$
    $$\text{Cost} = R 1960$$

**b) How much will she save if the exchange rate drops to $R 10$?**
*   **Solution:**
    $$\text{Cost} = 140 \times R 10$$
    $$\text{Cost} = R 1400$$
    $$\text{Saved} = R 1960 - R 1400$$
    $$\text{Saved} = R 560$$

**c) How much will she lose if the exchange rate moves to $R 20$?**
*   **Solution:**
    $$\text{Cost} = 140 \times R 20$$
    $$\text{Cost} = R 2800$$
    $$\text{Loss} = R 2800 - R 1960$$
    $$\text{Loss} = R 840$$

#### 39. Cross-Exchange Rates
Determine the exchange rate between the Australian Dollar (AUD) and the Japanese Yen (Yen).
*   **Given:** $100 \text{ Yen} = R 6,23$ and $1 \text{ AUD} = R 5,11$.

**Solution:**
$$\frac{\text{AUD}}{\text{Yen}} = \frac{\text{ZAR}}{\text{Yen}} \times \frac{\text{AUD}}{\text{ZAR}}$$
$$\frac{\text{AUD}}{\text{Yen}} = \frac{6,2287}{100} \times \frac{1}{5,1094}$$
$$\frac{\text{AUD}}{\text{Yen}} = 0,012 \text{ AUD} = 1 \text{ Yen}$$
**Result:** $1 \text{ AUD} = 82,02 \text{ Yen}$.

#### 40. Compound Interest Investment
Khetang invests $€ 2850$ for 6 years at $5,3\%$ compound interest per annum.
*   **Exchange rate:** $€ 1 = R 12,89$.

**Solution:**
1.  **Convert Euros to Rand:** $2850 \times 12,89 = R 36 736,50$.
2.  **Calculate Compound Interest:**
    *   $P = 36 736,50$
    *   $i = 0,053$
    *   $n = 6$

    Using the formula $A = P(1 + i)^n$:
    $$A = 36 736,50(1 + 0,053)^6$$
    $$A = R 50 080,42$$

3.  **Calculate Interest Earned:**
    $$\text{Interest} = A - P$$
    $$\text{Interest} = 50 080,42 - 36 736,50 = R 13 343,92$$
