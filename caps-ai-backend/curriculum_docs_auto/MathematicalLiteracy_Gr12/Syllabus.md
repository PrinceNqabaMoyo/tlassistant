## 2. Content & Cognitive Scope
The curriculum focuses on the application of mathematical concepts to real-world contexts. Mastery is expected across four cognitive levels:

1.  **Knowledge:** Recall of definitions, formulas, and basic procedures.
2.  **Routine Procedures:** Application of standard algorithms (e.g., calculating simple interest, unit conversions).
3.  **Complex Procedures:** Multi-step problem solving requiring the integration of different sections (e.g., calculating tax on a loan repayment).
4.  **Problem Solving:** Non-routine problems requiring critical analysis and interpretation of data in context.

### Core Mathematical Concepts
*   **Finance:** Includes personal income tax calculations, banking interest (simple and compound), and currency conversion.
    *   *Compound Interest Formula:* $A = P(1 + i)^n$
*   **Measurement:** Focuses on spatial reasoning, including:
    *   Perimeter: $P = 2(l + w)$
    *   Area: $A = l \times w$ (rectangles), $A = \pi r^2$ (circles)
    *   Volume: $V = l \times w \times h$
*   **Data Handling & Probability:** Analysis of trends, interpretation of graphs, and calculation of likelihoods.

## 3. Assessment Matrix & Exam Weightings
*Note: While specific percentage weightings per paper are governed by the National Protocol for Assessment (NPA), the Grade 12 curriculum is structured to ensure a balance between Paper 1 (Basic Skills) and Paper 2 (Application/Problem Solving).*

| Assessment Component | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Formal Assignments** | Focus on research, data collection, and application of theory to specific scenarios. |
| **Formal Investigations** | Focus on extended problem-solving and mathematical modelling of real-world phenomena. |
| **Paper 1** | Focuses on content from Finance, Measurement, and Data Handling (Routine/Complex procedures). |
| **Paper 2** | Focuses on Maps, Plans, and integrated application across all topics (Complex/Problem solving). |

*   **Pacing Guidelines:** Teachers are expected to follow the sequence provided in the table above to ensure all topics are covered prior to the final National Senior Certificate (NSC) examinations.
*   **Depth of Treatment:** All topics must be treated with a focus on "Mathematical Literacy," meaning the emphasis is on the *interpretation* of the result rather than purely abstract calculation.
