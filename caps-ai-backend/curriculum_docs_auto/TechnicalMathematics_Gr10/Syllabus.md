### 4. Mathematical Examples & Formulas

**Algebraic Operations:**
*   Subtraction: $5a + 10b - (4a + 8b)$
*   Binomial expansion: $(x + 2)(x - 2) = x^2 - 4$
*   Square of a binomial: $(4x - 5y)^2 = 16x^2 - 40xy + 25y^2$
*   Binomial product: $(x - y)(2x - 3y) = 2x^2 - 5xy + 3y^2$
*   Polynomial product: $(2a + 3)(a^2 - 2a - 1) = 2a^3 - a^2 - 8a - 3$

**Factorisation Examples:**
*   Common factors: $2x^2y + 4xy^2 - 6xy = 2xy(x + 2y - 3)$
*   Grouping: $ab + ac - 2b - 2c = a(b + c) - 2(b + c) = (a - 2)(b + c)$
*   Difference of squares: $2a^2 - 8b^2 = 2(a^2 - 4b^2) = 2(a - 2b)(a + 2b)$
*   Difference of cubes: $x^3 - 8y^3 = (x - 2y)(x^2 + 2xy + 4y^2)$
*   Trinomials: $x^2 - 7x - 8 = (x - 8)(x + 1)$

#### 2. Content & Cognitive Scope

**Core Concepts & Skills:**
*   **Trigonometric Ratios:** Definition of $\sin \theta$, $\cos \theta$, and $\tan \theta$ using right-angled triangles for $0^\circ \leq \theta \leq 360^\circ$.
*   **Reciprocal Ratios:** Mastery of:
    *   $\csc \theta = \frac{1}{\sin \theta}$
    *   $\sec \theta = \frac{1}{\cos \theta}$
    *   $\cot \theta = \frac{1}{\tan \theta}$
*   **Quadrant Analysis:** Calculating ratios in specific quadrants using diagrams and Pythagoras' theorem.
*   **Equations:** Solving simple trigonometric equations for angles between $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$.
*   **Applications:** Solving two-dimensional problems involving right-angled triangles.
*   **Graphical Representation:** 
    *   Plotting $y = a \sin \theta$, $y = a \cos \theta$, and $y = a \tan \theta$ for $0^\circ \leq \theta \leq 360^\circ$.
    *   Investigating transformations: $y = a \sin \theta + q$ and $y = a \cos \theta + q$.
    *   Identifying asymptotes, axes of symmetry, domain, and range.

**Cognitive Levels:**
*   **Knowledge (K):** Recall of definitions and basic ratio identities.
*   **Routine Procedure (R):** Calculator usage, standard graph plotting, and basic equation solving.
*   **Complex Procedure (C):** Determining values using Pythagoras in quadrants, identifying graph properties (asymptotes/symmetry), and investigating the effects of parameters $a$ and $q$.

### Content & Cognitive Scope

#### 1. Analytical Geometry
*   **Core Concepts:** Cartesian plane, distance formula, gradient, mid-point, linear equations.
*   **Skills:**
    *   Calculating distance between two points.
    *   Determining gradients and identifying parallel/perpendicular lines.
    *   Finding the mid-point of a line segment.
    *   Deriving the equation of a straight line passing through two points.
*   **Depth of Treatment:** Students must be able to apply algebraic methods to geometric problems.

#### 2. Functions and Graphs
*   **Core Concepts:** Functional notation, independent/dependent variables, domain, range, asymptotes.
*   **Functions to Master:**
    *   Linear: $y = mx + c$
    *   Quadratic: $y = ax^2 + q$ (where $a = \pm 1$)
    *   Hyperbola: $y = \frac{a}{x}$
    *   Exponential: $y = ab^x$ (where $b \neq 1$ and $b > 0$)
*   **Skills:**
    *   Point-by-point plotting.
    *   Identifying $x$-intercepts, $y$-intercepts, turning points, and axes of symmetry.
    *   Investigating the effect of parameter $q$ on graph translation.
    *   Reading values from graphs.
*   **Parameters:** $a, b, c, m, p, q \in \mathbb{R}$.

### Content & Cognitive Scope

#### Core Concepts & Skills
*   **Congruency:** Investigation of conditions for two triangles to be congruent:
    *   Side-Side-Side (SSS)
    *   Side-Angle-Side (SAS)
    *   Angle-Angle-Corresponding Side (AAS)
    *   Right Angle-Hypotenuse-Side (RHS)
*   **Similarity:** Investigation of conditions for similarity; identifying corresponding angles and calculating ratios of corresponding sides.
*   **Quadrilaterals:** Practical application of properties of special quadrilaterals.
*   **Pythagoras' Theorem:** 
    *   Formula: $$c^2 = a^2 + b^2$$ (where $c$ is the hypotenuse).
    *   Application: Solving for unknown sides in right-angled triangles.

#### Cognitive Levels
*   **Knowledge (K):** Recall of definitions and properties of triangles and quadrilaterals.
*   **Routine Procedure (R):** Calculation of unknown angles and side lengths using established theorems and properties.
*   **Complex Procedure (C):** Investigating geometric conjectures and applying multiple properties to solve practical problems.
*   **Problem Solving (P):** Application of geometric principles to non-routine practical scenarios.
