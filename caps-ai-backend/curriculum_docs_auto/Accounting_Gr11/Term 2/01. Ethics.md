## 1. Code of Ethics

*   **Definition:** A Code of ethics is a statement of norms and beliefs of the business, describing acceptable behaviour in the workplace.
*   **Purpose:** It enables every person involved in the business to know and understand what is considered right and wrong in specific situations.
*   **Core Values:** Most codes will value honesty, integrity, and diversity.

---

## 2. Basic Principles of Ethics

### 2.1 Ethical Conduct

*   **Definition of Ethics:** Ethics is defined as a set of moral principles of a profession according to which a person should behave.
*   **Responsibility:** Both employees and employers have a responsibility towards themselves, fellow colleagues, clients, and the profession to act morally and ethically.
*   **Standardization:** In any profession, a set of rules should be laid down so that everyone knows what is acceptable.
*   **Inclusivity:** 
    *   An ethical code of conduct should be drawn up in such a way that subordinates do not feel that rules are applicable only to them.
    *   Both employees and employers should be involved in setting up a code of conduct for a business.

## 2.2 Leadership
*   **Definition:** Leadership is a position or state of being in control of a group of people or an organisation.
*   **Role of a Leader:** A leader must take control, and others must follow.
*   **Effective Leadership:** Good leadership is demonstrated when followers obey the leader due to respect for the leader's words and actions.

## 2.3 Discipline
*   **Workplace Conduct:** Employees are required to work and behave in a controlled manner by obeying specific rules.
*   **Code of Ethics:** This document outlines acceptable workplace behaviour and the consequences for violating these standards.
*   **Enforcement:** Employees who fail to adhere to the Code of Ethics will be subject to disciplinary action.

## 2.4 Transparency
*   **Definition:** Transparency refers to behaviour that is open and honest, ensuring that there is nothing to hide.

## 2.5 Accountability
*   **Definition:** Being accountable means taking full responsibility for one's words and actions, and being able to justify those actions to others.

## 2.6 Fairness
*   **Definition:** Fairness is the quality of being reasonable and just.
*   **Application:** It involves judging situations objectively, without bias or preconceived ideas.
*   **Customer Relations:** Customers and clients must perceive that they are being treated fairly by the business.

## 2.7 Sustainability
*   **Definition:** Businesses must operate in a way that demonstrates respect for the environment and the responsible use of resources.
*   **Long-term Impact:** Sustainability focuses on making a positive difference in the long term by avoiding waste and preventing environmental damage.

## 2.8 Responsible Management
Responsible management is guided by the "3 Ps," which ensure a business operates ethically and sustainably:

*   **People:** A responsible manager considers all stakeholders (employees, suppliers, and customers) and treats them ethically.
*   **Planet:** A responsible manager considers the environment and takes active steps to ensure it is protected rather than destroyed.
*   **Profit:** A responsible manager ensures the business achieves maximum profit, but only through the use of ethical business practices.

---

## 2.9 Integrity
Integrity is defined as honesty, efficiency, sincerity, honesty towards oneself, and the upholding of values and norms. Business success relies on employees and clients respecting the integrity of the business, which starts with its leaders.

### Why Integrity is Important for Managers:
1.  **Builds Confidence:** When managers do not use their positions for personal enrichment, it fosters loyalty and support from others.
2.  **Influences Others:** A manager's conduct sets the tone for the entire organization. The character of the manager determines the character of the business.
3.  **Creates High Standards:** A manager with integrity sets a positive example for employees to follow. As the saying goes: "People do what people see."

---

## 2.10 Confidentiality
Confidentiality refers to the practice of keeping sensitive business information private. Employees must not disclose information intended only for the business to outside parties.

*   **Example:** If a manager is negotiating a large contract, that information must remain private. If it is leaked, a rival business could use that information to win the contract instead.
*   **Consequence:** Employees who share confidential information are considered disloyal to the business.

## 2.11 Objectivity
**Definition:** Objectivity is the ability to act in an unbiased way.

*   **Key Principle:** Decisions must be based on true facts rather than personal feelings, preferences, or the influence of others.
*   **Application:** An objective person remains fair and impartial when carrying out their professional duties.

## 2.12 Professional Ability and Proper Care
When providing professional services, individuals are expected to apply their knowledge, skills, experience, and diligence.

*   **Competence:** Professionals should not offer services for which they are not qualified. If a task falls outside their expertise, they must seek appropriate advice and assistance.
*   **Continuous Development:** Professionals must maintain a high standard of knowledge and skills. This ensures that clients and employers receive services that align with the most recent practical, legal, and technical developments.

## 2.13 Professional Conduct
This concept relates to the behavior of all individuals connected to an enterprise.

*   **Workplace Standards:** Every person must conduct themselves in a manner that does not negatively influence the enterprise.
*   **Interpersonal Expectations:** Employees are expected to be polite and show consideration toward all parties with whom they interact within the business environment.

# Term 1 Topic 2: Ethics

## 2.14 Technical Standards

Employees are required to possess all relevant technical and professional skills necessary for their specific positions. All tasks assigned by an employer must be executed with integrity and must strictly conform to:
*   Technical and professional standards established by the enterprise.
*   Requirements set by relevant authorities.
*   All applicable legislation.

### Important Notes
*   Ethics is an integrated topic and will be continuously assessed throughout the Study Guide.
*   When answering ethics-related questions, adopt the mindset of an employee within a business and apply the studied principles to workplace scenarios.
