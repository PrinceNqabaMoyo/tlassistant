### 2. CONTENT & COGNITIVE SCOPE

#### Topic: Algebraic Expressions
*   **Core Concepts:**
    *   Real numbers (rational vs. irrational).
    *   Rounding to appropriate degrees of accuracy.
    *   Multiplication of binomials by trinomials.
    *   Factorisation: Trinomials, grouping in pairs, sum and difference of two cubes.
    *   Simplification of algebraic fractions (limited to sum and difference of cubes).
*   **Cognitive Levels & Depth:**
    *   **Knowledge (K):** Identifying perfect squares.
    *   **Routine Procedures (R):** Standard factorisation, basic simplification of fractions.
    *   **Complex Procedures (C):** Simplifying complex algebraic fractions involving sum/difference of cubes.

#### Topic: Exponents
*   **Core Concepts:**
    *   Laws of exponents: $x^m \cdot x^n = x^{m+n}$, $x^m \div x^n = x^{m-n}$, $(x^m)^n = x^{mn}$, $x^m \cdot y^m = (xy)^m$.
    *   Definitions: $x^{-n} = \frac{1}{x^n}$ ($x \neq 0$), $x^0 = 1$ ($x \neq 0$).
    *   Application: Simplifying expressions and solving exponential equations where $m, n \in \mathbb{Q}$.
*   **Cognitive Levels & Depth:**
    *   **Routine Procedures (R):** Applying laws to simplify expressions like $(3 \times 5^2)^3 - 75$.
    *   **Complex Procedures (C):** Solving equations involving exponential terms, e.g., $3^{x+1} + 3^{x-1} = 10$.

### 3. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS & NOTATION

**Algebraic Expressions:**
*   Factorisation examples: $m^2 - 2m + 1$, $2x^2 - x - 3$, $\frac{y^2}{2} - \frac{13y}{2} + 18$.
*   Fractional simplification: $\frac{1-2x}{4x^2-1} + \frac{1}{2x^2-3x+1}$.

**Exponents:**
*   Product Law: $x^m \cdot x^n = x^{m+n}$
*   Quotient Law: $x^m \div x^n = x^{m-n}$
*   Power of a Power: $(x^m)^n = x^{mn}$
*   Power of a Product: $x^m \cdot y^m = (xy)^m$
*   Negative Exponent: $x^{-n} = \frac{1}{x^n}$
*   Zero Exponent: $x^0 = 1$
*   Exponential Equations: $2^x = 0,125$ and $3^{x+1} + 3^{x-1} = 10$

### 4. Clarification & Mathematical Specifications

#### Key Pedagogical Notes
*   Similarity of triangles is fundamental to trigonometric ratios.
*   Ratios are independent of side lengths and depend only on angles.
*   Doubling an angle has a different effect from doubling the ratio (e.g., $2\sin \theta \neq \sin 2\theta$).

#### Mathematical Examples & Formulas
*   **Equation Solving:** Solve for $x$ where $x \in [0^\circ; 90^\circ]$:
    $$4\sin x - 1 = 3$$
*   **Identity Application:** Calculate without a calculator:
    $$5\sin \theta + 4 = 0 \quad (0^\circ \leq \theta \leq 270^\circ)$$
*   **Geometric Application:** 
    *   Given rectangle $ABCD$ with $AB = 2\text{cm}$.
    *   Given $B\hat{E}C = 75^\circ$.
    *   Determine the area of the rectangle.
    *   Determine the length of the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle $ABC$ where $\hat{B} = 90^\circ$, $\hat{A} = 30^\circ$, and $AB = 10\text{cm}$.

### 2. CONTENT & COGNITIVE SCOPE

#### Core Concepts
*   **Definition of a Function:** Understanding the relationship where an input value uniquely determines an output value.
*   **Representations:** Working flexibly between tables, graphs, words, and formulae.
*   **Basic Graphs:**
    *   Parabola: $f(x) = x^2$
    *   Hyperbola: $f(x) = \frac{1}{x}$
    *   Exponential: $f(x) = b^x$ ($b > 0, b \neq 1$)
    *   Trigonometric: $y = \sin \theta$, $y = \cos \theta$, $y = \sin \theta$
*   **Transformations:** Investigating the effects of parameters $a$ and $q$ on the graphs defined by $y = a \cdot f(x) + q$.
*   **Trigonometric Functions:**
    *   $y = a \cos \theta + q$
    *   $y = a \tan \theta + q$ (where $a, q \in \mathbb{Q}$)
    *   Domain: $\theta \in [0^\circ; 360^\circ]$

#### Skills to be Mastered
*   **Point-by-point plotting:** Constructing graphs from basic principles.
*   **Feature Identification:** Determining shape, domain, range, axes of symmetry, turning points, and intercepts.
*   **Parameter Analysis:** Determining the effects of vertical stretch ($a$) and vertical shift ($q$).
*   **Interpretation:** Sketching graphs from equations and finding equations from given graphs.

#### Cognitive Levels & Depth
*   **Knowledge:** Recall of basic graph shapes and definitions of functions.
*   **Routine Procedures:** Plotting points, identifying intercepts, and applying standard transformations.
*   **Complex Procedures:** Analyzing the combined effects of parameters $a$ and $q$ on various function types.
*   **Problem Solving:** Interpreting graphs and deriving equations based on observed features.

#### 3. Mathematical Formulas
The following formulas are required for mastery within the specified topics:

**Analytical Geometry:**
*   Distance formula: $d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$
*   Gradient formula: $m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$
*   Mid-point formula: $M = \left( \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2} ; \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2} \right)$

**Finance and Growth:**
*   Simple growth: $A = P(1 + in)$
*   Compound growth: $A = P(1 + i)^n$

#### 4. Mathematical Formulas & Notation
*   **Scaling Factor:** If a dimension is multiplied by $k$, the volume $V$ changes by a factor of $k^3$ (for 3D) and surface area $SA$ changes by a factor of $k^2$ (for 2D surfaces).
*   **Composite Solids:** 
    *   Volume of a cylinder: $V = \pi r^2 h$
    *   Surface area of a cylinder: $SA = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh$
    *   General approach for composite figures: $V_{total} = \sum V_{parts}$ and $SA_{total} = \sum SA_{exposed\_surfaces}$.

### 2. Content & Cognitive Scope

**Core Concepts:**
*   **Relative vs. Theoretical Probability:** Understanding that relative frequency approaches theoretical probability as the number of trials increases.
*   **Set Theory in Probability:** Using Venn diagrams to represent sample spaces ($S$) and events ($A, B$).
*   **Probability Laws:**
    *   Addition Rule: $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$
    *   Mutually Exclusive Events: $P(A \cap B) = 0$
    *   Complementary Events: $P(A) + P(A') = 1$

**Cognitive Depth:**
*   **Knowledge:** Recall of probability definitions and notation.
*   **Routine Procedures:** Calculating relative frequency and applying basic probability formulas.
*   **Complex Procedures:** Constructing and interpreting Venn diagrams to solve multi-step problems involving overlapping sets.
*   **Problem Solving:** Analyzing survey data to extract set information and calculating probabilities based on Venn diagram representations.
