# **GLOSSARY** 

|**Alpha Centauri:**|our second closest _easily visible_ star after the Sun;|
|---|---|
||it is actually two stars orbiting very close together|
|**amplifier:**|a device which amplifies (to make something<br>bigger) the radio wave signals|
|**antenna:**|the dish or other device used to collect radio waves<br>in a radio telescope|
|**asteroid belt:**|the area where most asteroids are found in our<br>solar system, lying between the orbits of Mars and<br>Jupiter|
|**asteroid:**|a small rocky object orbiting the Sun|
|**astronomical unit**<br>**(AU):**|the average distance between the Earth and the<br>Sun, equal to around 150 million kilometres|
|**celestial:**|positioned in or relating to the sky, or outer space<br>as observed in astronomy|
|**chromatic aberration:**|an optical effect where different colours are<br>refracted by different amounts in a lens leading to<br>a distorted image|
|**comet:**|a small object made of ice and dust which|
||**.**<br>sometimes enters the inner solar system; when a<br>comet enters the inner solar system, part of it<br>evaporates to form a long tail of ice and dust<br>pointing away from the Sun|
|**constellation:**|a group of stars that form a pattern in the sky when<br>viewed from Earth|
|**convection:**|one of the three ways to transport heat energy (the<br>other two are conduction and radiation); as a liquid<br>or gas is heated, it becomes less dense and rises;<br>while denser colder material sinks, creating a flow<br>of moving liquid or gas which transports heat<br>energy along with it|
|**dwarf planet:**|a large, roughly spherical object orbiting a star<br>which cannot be classed as a planet because it is<br>not large enough to sweep out other objects from<br>its orbit|
|**filament:**|a threadlike structure in space containing galaxies<br>and galaxy groups and clusters|
|**galaxy bulge:**|a spheroidal (rugby ball shaped) distribution of old<br>stars at the centre of a galaxy|
|**galaxy cluster:**|a collection of over 50 or more galaxies, held<br>together by gravity|
|**galaxy disk:**|the flat distribution of stars, gas and dust in a<br>galaxy|
|**galaxy group:**|a collection of about 50 or less galaxies, held<br>together by gravity|
|**galaxy:**|a collection of millions or billions of stars, gas and<br>dust all held together by gravity|

|**gas giant:**|a large planet made mostly of gas with no solid<br>surface; the four outermost planets in the solar<br>system are gas giants|
|---|---|
|**habitable zone:**|the region surrounding a star in which water can<br>remain in its liquid state|
|**Kuiper Belt:**|region of space filled with trillions of small objects<br>that lie in the outer reaches of the solar system,<br>past the orbit of Neptune|
|**Kuiper Belt object:**|a small icy object orbiting the Sun out beyond the<br>orbit of Neptune|
|**light hour:**|the distance that light travels in one hour|
|**light minute:**|the distance that light travels in one minute|
|**light year:**|the distance that light travels in one year|
|**nuclear fusion:**|the process by which stars produce their energy;<br>light atomic nuclei come together and merge to<br>form heavier atomic nuclei, releasing energy as<br>they do so; in the Sun, hydrogen nuclei fuse with<br>other hydrogen nuclei to form heavier helium nuclei|
|**Oort Cloud:**|a hypothetical huge cloud of icy objects (comets)<br>surrounding the Sun at the very edge of our solar<br>system at a distance between 5,000 and 100,000<br>times the Earth's distance from the Sun|
|**photosynthesis:**|the process by which green plants and some other<br>organisms use sunlight to synthesise foods from<br>carbon dioxide and water producing oxygen as a<br>byproduct|
|**primary mirror:**|**.**<br>the light-collecting mirror in an optical telescope|
|**Proxima Centauri:**|our second closest star after the Sun|
|**receiver:**|a device that detects radio wave signals|
|**SALT:**|the Southern African Large Telescope, the largest<br>optical telescope in the southern hemisphere|
|**SKA:**|the Square Kilometre Array, the largest planned<br>radio telescope array in the world|
|**solar system:**|the Sun, and the collection of planets and smaller<br>objects that orbit around the Sun|
|**solar wind:**|the continuous flow of charged particles from the<br>Sun that extends out to the far reaches of the solar<br>system|
|**spiral arm:**|a region of stars, gas and dust forming a curved<br>shape spiralling out from the centre of a spiral<br>galaxy|
|**star:**|a huge ball of burning gas which emits energy in<br>the form of light and heat|
|**starlore:**|mythical stories about the stars, planets and<br>constellations|
|**sunspot:**|a dark region or spot which appears on the surface<br>of the Sun from time to time; sunspots are cooler<br>than the rest of the Sun's surface|
|**telescope:**|an instrument used to look at distant objects, which<br>makes distant objects appear brighter, larger and<br>clearer; optical telescopes collect visible light and<br>radio telescopes collect radio waves|

|**terrestrial planet:**|a planet with a rocky surface like the Earth's<br>surface; the four innermost planets in the solar<br>system are terrestrial planets|
|---|---|
|**Universe:**|**.**<br>all of existence, including all planets, stars, galaxies,<br>the space between objects, and all matter and<br>energy|
|**void:**|a vast empty bubble in space found between<br>filaments|
