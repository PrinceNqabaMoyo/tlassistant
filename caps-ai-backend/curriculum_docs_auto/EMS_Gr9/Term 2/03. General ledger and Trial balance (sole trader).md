# Financial Literacy: Posting Cash Transactions to the General Ledger

## Introduction to the Topic

In this topic, learners will explore **Financial Literacy**, focusing on:
* Posting cash transactions of a trading business from the **Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ)** and **Cash Payments Journal (CPJ)** to the **General Ledger**.
* Preparing a **Trial Balance** of a trading business.

---

## Key Concepts & Discussions

* **Stock Control:** Discuss the concept of stock control and why it is important in a business.
* **Layout Revision:** Revise the layouts of the General Ledger and the Trial Balance with learners.

---

## Summary of General Ledger Postings and Account Treatments

* **Total of the Bank (R3 300):** Bank is **credited**, because it is an Asset that has decreased.
* **Total of Stationery (R350):** Stationery is **debited**, because it is an expense that has decreased Owner's equity on the debit side.
* **Total of Trading stock (R2 400):** Trading Stock is **debited**, because it is an Asset that has increased.
* **Total of Sundry accounts:** Each entry is posted individually, not the total.
* **Telephone (R550):** Debited, because it is an expense that has decreased Owner's equity.

# Accounting Revision Notes: Posting Journals to the General Ledger

## 1. Core Guidelines for Posting

### Posting from the Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ)
The CRJ is the journal where receipts are entered, which increases cash in the business's bank account. There are two ways to explain this process:
* **Rule-based approach:** Bank has been debited because it has increased (Assets increase on the debit side) and, according to the double-entry principle, all other accounts (EXCEPT Cost of Sales) should be credited.
* **Identification approach:** Identify each account as shown in the ledger examples.

### Posting from the Cash Payments Journal (CPJ)
The CPJ is the journal where payments are entered, which decreases cash in the business's bank account. There are two ways to explain this process:
* **Rule-based approach:** The bank has been credited because it has decreased (Assets decrease on the credit side) and, according to the double-entry principle, all other accounts should be debited.
* **Identification approach:** Identify each account as shown in the ledger examples.

### General Ledger Formatting and Rules
* **Heading:** The heading of the ledger contains **ONLY** the name of the business, **NOT** the date.
* **Date:** Analysis columns are posted on the **last day of the specific month**. Entries in the **Sundry accounts** are posted on the **day that the transaction occurs**.
* **Folios:** When a transaction is posted, the folio of each ledger account must be entered in the journal. 
  * In the Sundry accounts column, the folio is entered in the folio column provided.
  * In the analysis columns, the folio is entered below the total of each column. 
  * Learners cannot enter the folio numbers if the completed journal appears only in the Learner's Book. However, all other folios must be entered.
* **Balancing:** Regularly revise the balancing and closing off of General Ledger accounts.
