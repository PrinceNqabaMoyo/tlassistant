# Financial Literacy: Recording Transactions and Posting

## Overview of Topics

In this topic, you will learn about **Financial Literacy**, focusing on:
* Recording cash and credit transactions of a sole trader in subsidiary journals.
* Posting from the Debtors Journal (DJ), Creditors Journal (CJ), and General Ledger.
* Preparing a Trial Balance.

## Reinforcing Subsidiary Journals

Learners should already know how to record various transactions in journals and post them to the General Ledger, Debtors Ledger, and Creditors Ledger. 

### 1. Source Documents by Journal

Each journal is distinguished by the specific source document used:

| CRJ (Cash Receipts Journal) | CPJ (Cash Payments Journal) | DJ (Debtors Journal) | CJ (Creditors Journal) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cash register roll<br>Duplicate receipt<br>Deposit slip | Cheque counterfoil | Duplicate invoice issued | Original invoice received |

### 2. Keywords Indicating Specific Journals

Transactions often contain keywords that indicate which journal should be used:

| CRJ | CPJ | DJ | CJ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cash received | Cash paid | Credit sales | Credit purchases |

---

## Posting to Subsidiary Ledgers

Reinforce the differences between posting to the Debtors Ledger and Creditors Ledger:

| Debtors Ledger: | Creditors Ledger: |
| :--- | :--- |
| $\text{DJ} \rightarrow \text{Invoice} \rightarrow \text{Dr} +$ | $\text{CJ} \rightarrow \text{Invoice} \rightarrow \text{Cr} +$ |
| $\text{CRJ} \rightarrow \text{Receipt} \rightarrow \text{Cr} -$ | $\text{CPJ} \rightarrow \text{Cheque} \rightarrow \text{Dr} -$ |

---

## General Ledger Posting Guide

The following summary assists with posting from journals to the General Ledger:

| Journal | Posting |
| :--- | :--- |
| **CRJ** | **Debit:** Bank<br>**Credit:** All the other accounts except for Cost of Sales |
| **CPJ** | **Credit:** Bank<br>**Debit:** All the other accounts |
| **DJ** | **Debit:** Debtors Control<br>**Credit:** Sales<br>**Debit:** Cost of Sales<br>**Credit:** Trading Stock |
| **CJ** | **Credit:** Creditors Control<br>**Debit:** All the other accounts |

### Concepts & Structure

This section covers accounting source documents, subsidiary journals, and the analysis of financial transactions according to the South African CAPS curriculum for Grade 10-11 Accounting.

#### Key Concepts
* **Subsidiary Journals:** Books of prime entry used to record specific types of business transactions before they are posted to the general ledger.
  * **Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ):** Used to record all cash received by the business.
* **Mark-up:** The percentage added to the cost price of goods to determine the selling price.
  * Formula: $\text{Selling Price} = \text{Cost Price} + \text{Mark-up}$
