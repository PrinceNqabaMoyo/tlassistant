---

## 2. Key Concepts & Definitions

### Debtors and Credit Sales
* **Debtor:** A person or business that owes money to the business because goods or services were bought on credit.
* **Credit Sales:** Transactions where goods are sold to customers with the agreement that payment will be made at a later date. Only **credit sales** are entered into the Debtors Journal (DJ).
* **Debtors Allowance:** An allowance or reduction granted to debtors (often due to damaged or incorrect goods returned), and why businesses offer this option to maintain customer relations.
* **Advantages and Disadvantages of Credit:** Discuss the benefits of credit to businesses (increasing sales volume) and customers, as well as the risks (bad debts, cash flow delays).

### The National Credit Act (NCA)
* An act designed to protect consumers and regulate the credit industry.
* **Key Purpose:** Discourages reckless lending and protects both borrowers and lenders.

---

## 3. Recording Transactions in Journals

### The Debtors Journal (DJ)
* **Definition:** A subsidiary journal used exclusively for recording sales of goods on credit.
* **Source Document:** Duplicate invoice issued to a debtor for goods sold.
* **Recording Process:** Each transaction (invoice number) is recorded in the **Sales** column and the **Cost of sales** column.
* **Control Check:** When the journal is completed, the total of the **Cost of sales** column must equal the calculated cost price of the total **Sales** column.

### The Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ)
* **Definition:** A subsidiary journal used to record all receipts of money into the business.
* **Application for Debtors:** When a debtor pays their account, the amount is entered into the **Debtors control** column of the CRJ.
* **Source Document:** Receipt issued to a debtor for payment received.

### The General Ledger & Debtors Control
* **Debtors Control Account:** Opened in the General Ledger when credit sales to debtors are entered (from the DJ) and when payments from debtors are entered (from the CRJ). It controls all individual transactions of debtors in the Debtors Ledger.
