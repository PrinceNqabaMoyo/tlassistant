### 4. MATHEMATICAL CONTEXTUALIZATION
Mathematical Literacy assessments in these grades require the application of formulas related to the topics above. Examples of required calculations include:

*   **Simple Interest:** $A = P(1 + i \times n)$
*   **Compound Interest:** $A = P(1 + i)^n$
*   **Break-even Point:** $Total\ Revenue = Total\ Cost$
*   **Area of a Rectangle:** $Area = length \times width$
*   **Volume of a Rectangular Prism:** $Volume = length \times width \times height$
