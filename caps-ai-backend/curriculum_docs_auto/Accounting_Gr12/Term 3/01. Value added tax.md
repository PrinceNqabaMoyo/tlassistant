# Term 4 Topic 1: Value Added Tax (VAT)

## The VAT System

*   **VAT:** Value Added Tax.
*   **Role of Businesses:** Businesses act as agents for the government. They collect VAT on behalf of the government and pay it to the South African Revenue Service (SARS), which is the body responsible for the administration of VAT.
*   **Registration Thresholds:**
    *   Businesses with a turnover of $R50\,000$ per year may choose to register for VAT.
    *   Businesses with a turnover of $R1\,000\,000$ per year must register for VAT.
*   **Input Tax:** Tax paid by the business on purchases or services provided to the business. The business can claim this tax back from SARS.
*   **Output Tax:** Tax received on sales or services provided by the business. This must be paid to SARS.
*   **General Rule:** If the business benefits financially from a transaction, the business owes VAT to SARS. If the benefit decreases, the business owes less to SARS.

---

## VAT Transaction Analysis

The following table outlines how specific transactions affect the VAT amount owed to or claimable from SARS:

| Transaction | VAT amount increases or decreases |
| :--- | :--- |
| Credit balance on Output VAT account | Increases |
| Sales to customers | Increases |
| Services rendered to customers | Increases |
| Drawings by owner | Increases |
| Discount allowed to debtors | Decreases |
| Merchandise, equipment returned to creditors | Increases |
| Debit balance on Input VAT account | Decreases |
| Bad debts written off | Decreases |
| Bad debts recovered | Increases |
| Returns by debtors | Decreases |
| Trading stock, equipment purchased from creditors | Decreases |
| Discount received from creditors | Increases |

## Exercise 1: Calculating VAT Owed to SARS

### Key Concepts & Rules
*   **VAT Rate:** In South Africa, the standard VAT rate is $15\%$.
*   **VAT Inclusive:** To calculate the VAT portion from an inclusive amount, use: $\text{Amount} \times \frac{15}{115}$.
*   **VAT Exclusive:** To calculate the VAT portion from an exclusive amount, use: $\text{Amount} \times \frac{15}{100}$.
*   **Output VAT:** VAT collected on sales (increases amount owed to SARS).
*   **Input VAT:** VAT paid on purchases (decreases amount owed to SARS).

### Transactions for the Two-Month Period
*Note: Amounts include VAT unless otherwise stated.*

1.  **Cash sales:** $R98\,040$ (Output VAT)
2.  **Credit sales:** $R57\,000$ (Output VAT)
3.  **Cash purchases:** $R48\,000$ (Excl. VAT) (Input VAT)
4.  **Credit purchases:** $R109\,440$ (Input VAT)

## Exercise 2: Modiba Traders VAT Calculation

### Scenario
Modiba Traders is a sole proprietor registered for VAT under category B on the invoice basis. The following figures for May and June 20.6 include VAT where applicable (VAT rate = $15\%$).

| Item | Amount (Incl. VAT) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sales** | |
| Sales invoices issued | R37 821 |
| Cash sales | R22 965 |
| Redundant computer sold | R2 500 |
| Insurance payout (stolen truck) | R40 000 |
| **Expenses** | |
| New computer purchased | R12 000 |
| Stock and overheads | R20 000 |
| Credit notes issued | R1 580 |

---

### Instructions
Calculate the amount that Modiba Traders owes to SARS.

### Notes for Calculation
1. **VAT Output:** Calculated on all sales and income. Note that insurance payouts are generally considered "outside the scope" of VAT or exempt, depending on the specific policy nature; however, in standard school exercises, they are often excluded from VAT calculations unless specified as a taxable supply.
2. **VAT Input:** Calculated on all business expenses.
3. **Credit Notes:** These reduce the VAT output (as they represent a reversal of a sale).
4. **Formula:** To extract VAT from an inclusive amount, use:
   $$\text{VAT} = \text{Amount} \times \frac{15}{115}$$

### Calculation Table

| Description | Amount (Incl. VAT) | VAT Output (15/115) | VAT Input (15/115) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sales invoices | R37 821 | R4 933,35 | |
| Cash sales | R22 965 | R2 996,09 | |
| Redundant computer | R2 500 | R326,09 | |
| Credit notes issued | (R1 580) | (R206,09) | |
| New computer | R12 000 | | R1 565,22 |
| Stock and overheads | R20 000 | | R2 608,70 |
| **Totals** | | **R8 049,44** | **R4 173,92** |

### Final Calculation
To find the amount payable to SARS:
$$\text{Net VAT Payable} = \text{Total VAT Output} - \text{Total VAT Input}$$
$$\text{Net VAT Payable} = R8\,049,44 - R4\,173,92$$
$$\text{Net VAT Payable} = R3\,875,52$$

**Modiba Traders owes SARS R3 875,52.**
