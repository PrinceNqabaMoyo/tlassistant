# **GLOSSARY** 

|**altitude:**|height above sea level|
|---|---|
|**atmosphere:**|the layer of gases surrounding the Earth and held in<br>place by gravity|
|**aurora:**|a natural phenomenon whereby charged particles<br>from the Sun interact with atmospheric particles;<br>this is observed as bright, coloured "lights" in the<br>sky, mostly in polar regions|
|**bellows:**|a device that produces a stream of air when it is<br>squeezed|
|**biosphere:**|the part of the Earth and its atmosphere in which<br>living organisms exist or that is capable of<br>supporting life|
|**black dwarf:**|a white dwarf that has sufficiently cooled and used<br>up all of its energy so that it no longer emits any<br>heat or light; the star is now dead and will remain<br>like this|
|**blast furnace:**|used in the extraction of iron from iron-ore, a high<br>temperature oven in the form of a tower into which<br>compressed air can be introduced from below|
|**bloomery:**|a type of oven used for purifying iron from iron ore|
|**brittle:**|material that is hard, but can break or shatter easily|
|**carbon dioxide:**|**.**<br>a gas with the chemical formula $	ext{CO}_2$|
|**cementation:**|the process of solidifying sediments by chemical<br>compounds acting as glue|
|**CFCs:**|chlorofluorocarbons, are molecules which release<br>chlorine atoms due to solar radiation in the<br>stratosphere|
|**climate change:**|a significant and lasting change in weather<br>patterns; if there is a change in the world's weather<br>patterns, it is global climate change|
|**compaction:**|an increase in the density of something|
|**composition:**|what makes up a substance or a mixture|
|**constellation:**|a group of stars in a recognisable pattern|
|**continental crust:**|the thick part of the Earth's crust that forms the<br>continents|
|**cooling:**|lowering the temperature|
|**core:**|innermost layer of the Earth|
|**crust:**|the thin, solid, outermost layer of the Earth|
|**cycle:**|a continuous process where the last step feeds into<br>the first again|
|**density separation:**|a separation method where the differing densities<br>of particles are used to separate them out|
|**deposition:**|the process where sediments, rocks and and sand<br>are deposited (laid down) by wind or water|
|**electromagnets:**|a soft metal core made into a temporary magnet<br>by passing current through a coil surrounding it|

**336** Planet Earth and Beyond 

|**erosion:**|the breakdown and movement of the Earth's<br>surface by natural agents like wind and water|
|---|---|
|**evolution:**|(of stars) the changes a star undergoes as it is<br>born, lives and dies|
|**excavation:**|the process of removing rock containing ore from<br>the surrounding rock|
|**exosphere:**|considered part of outer space; the uppermost<br>layer directly above the thermosphere, where the<br>gases thin out and the atmosphere merges with<br>space|
|**exploration:**|the process of finding out where profitable mineral<br>deposits are located|
|**extrusive rock:**|igneous rock which forms when magma flows out<br>onto the surface of the Earth as lava|
|**flotation:**|a separation method by which hydrophobic<br>particles are separated from hydrophilic particles<br>by blowing air through the mixture|
|**geochemical**<br>**methods:**|exploration methods using knowledge of geology<br>and the chemistry of minerals|
|**geophysical methods:**|exploration methods using knowledge of geology<br>and the physical properties of minerals|
|**geosphere:**|the core, mantle and crust of the Earth|
|**global warming:**|a gradual increase in the temperature of the Earth's<br>atmosphere|
|**greenhouse effect:**|**.**<br>the trapping of the Sun's energy in the lower part<br>of the atmosphere due to the presence of<br>greenhouse gases|
|**greenhouse gases:**|gases like water vapour, carbon dioxide and<br>methane, which let through sunlight but reflect<br>ultraviolet radiation|
|**hydrosphere:**|all the water, in all its forms, found on Earth|
|**igneous rock:**|a rock type formed by magma or lava|
|**International Space**<br>**Station:**|a multinational space station, used for research<br>purposes, which orbits the Earth at 370 km above<br>the surface|
|**intrusive rock:**|igneous rock which forms from magma deep below<br>the surface of the Earth|
|**ionosphere:**|the region mainly in the thermosphere where high<br>energy light (UV light) can cause atoms, molecules<br>or substances to form an ion or ions, typically by<br>removing one or more electrons|
|**lithosphere:**|the outer part of the Earth consisting of the crust<br>and the upper part of the mantle; it includes all<br>rock, soil and minerals found on Earth|
|**magnetic separation:**|a separation method based on the magnetic<br>properties of the mixture|
|**main sequence star:**|a star that has hydrogen undergoing nuclear fusion<br>reactions into helium in its core|
|**mantle:**|the middle layer of the Earth|
|**melting:**|the change from a solid to a liquid as a result of<br>heating|

|**mesosphere:**|the layer of the Earth's atmosphere above the<br>stratosphere, extending to about 80 km above the<br>surface of the Earth|
|---|---|
|**metamorphic rock:**|a rock type formed through the transformation, or<br>metamorphosis, of other rock types|
|**meteor:**|a small body of mass entering the Earth's<br>atmosphere from space which emits light as a<br>result of friction and heat, and appears as a streak<br>of light|
|**meteorite:**|a meteor which has collided with the Earth|
|**methane:**|a gas with the chemical formula $	ext{CH}_4$|
|**mineral:**|natural compounds formed through geological<br>processes; the term "mineral" includes both a<br>material's chemical composition and its structure|
|**nebula:**|a vast cloud of gas and dust in space|
|**neutron star:**|an extremely dense star made of neutrons about<br>the size of a small town in diameter|
|**northern lights:**|the aurora in the northern hemisphere; also called<br>aurora borealis|
|**nuclear fusion:**|process in which two light nuclei of atoms combine|
||to produce a heavier single nucleus, with a total<br>mass slightly less than that of the total initial<br>material, the difference in mass is radiated as<br>energy|
|**oceanic crust:**|**.**<br>the thinner part of the Earth's crust that underlies<br>the oceans|
|**ore:**|a naturally occurring solid material from which a<br>metal or valuable mineral can be extracted|
|**overburden:**|the layer of rock or sand overlying a mineral<br>deposit|
|**ozone:**|a gas molecule found in the stratosphere consisting<br>of 3 oxygen atoms (O3)|
|**panning:**|a separation method based on the density gradient<br>of the mixture|
|**PGM:**|platinum group metals, which includes ruthenium,<br>rhodium, palladium, osmium, iridium and platinum,<br>and are elements on the Periodic Table|
|**planetary nebula:**|a cloud of gas (the remains of the original star's<br>atmosphere) surrounding an old star; these have a<br>confusing name because they actually have<br>nothing to do with planets at all|
|**protostar:**|a contracting mass of gas that will become a star<br>once it is hot enough for nuclear fusion to start|
|**radiation:**|the transfer of energy from a source that does not<br>require physical contact or movement of particles|
|**red giant star:**|an old, bright, very big, cool star; main sequence<br>stars evolve to become red giant stars once the<br>hydrogen in their cores has been depleted|
|**rehabilitation:**|an area is restored to certain specifications, for<br>example an area that has been mined is<br>rehabilitated by planting trees or grass|

|**remote sensing:**|gathering information from a distance, without<br>making physical contact|
|---|---|
|**sediment:**|particles, for example those that arise from erosion<br>and weathering, settle in layers|
|**sedimentary rock:**|a rock type formed from solidifying sediment|
|**sedimentation:**|the deposition and solidification of sediment|
|**size separation:**|a separation method based on the size of the<br>particles|
|**slag:**|the waste product extracted from a blast furnace<br>after extracting iron from iron-ore|
|**slurry:**|a watery mixture of solids and liquids|
|**solidify:**|becoming a solid|
|**southern lights:**|the aurora in the southern hemisphere; also called<br>aurora australis|
|**stellar wind:**|a flow of neutral or charged gas ejected from a star<br>(the solar wind refers specifically to the stellar wind<br>of our Sun)|
|**stellar:**|of stars, such as a stellar nebula|
|**stratosphere:**|**.**<br>the layer of the Earth's atmosphere above the<br>troposphere, extending to about 50 km above the<br>surface of the Earth|
|**supernova:**|an explosion in a high mass star where the outer<br>layers of the star are flung off into space|
|**temperature gradient:**|how much the temperature changes as height<br>above sea level (altitude) increases|
|**thermosphere:**|the layer of the Earth's atmosphere above the<br>mesosphere, extending from about 480 to 600 km<br>above the surface of the Earth|
|**topsoil:**|the upper surface of the Earth consisting of a layer<br>of vegetation and soil|
|**troposphere:**|the lowest layer of the Earth's atmosphere,<br>extending from sea level to about 9-17 km|
|**water vapour:**|a gas with the chemical formula $	ext{H}_2O$; water in its<br>gaseous form|
|**weathering:**|the wearing away of rocks as a result of exposure<br>to wind, water and ice|
|**white dwarf:**|a small hot, very dense star that is the size of a<br>planet|
