|**electric cell:**|a system in which chemical reactions occur to<br>generate electricity|
|**electric charge:**|the physical property of matter that causes it to<br>experience a force when close to other electrically<br>charged matter; there are two types of electric<br>charges: positive and negative|
|**electric current:**|the rate of flow of charge in an electric circuit|
|**electrical appliance:**|an electrical device|
|**electrical power:**|the rate at which energy is transferred|
|**electrode:**|an electric conductor used to make contact with a<br>non-metal part of the circuit, such as a copper coin<br>or iron nail in a lemon, or zinc or copper plates in a<br>cell|
|**electrolyte:**|a special type of solution which is able to conduct<br>electricity|
|**electrostatic force:**|force of attraction or repulsion between<br>electrostatic charges|
