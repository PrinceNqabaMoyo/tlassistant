# Post-Exam Triage & Cognitive Repair Runbook

The Post-Exam Triage Engine transforms passive test scores into actionable, deterministic pedagogical interventions. Rather than telling a student *"You scored 52%"*, it informs them: *"You lost 16 marks purely due to `net_vs_gross_confusion`. Here is a 5-minute fix."*

## Diagnostic Autopsy Architecture

1. **Session Telemetry Aggregation (`session_index` Firestore collection):**
   - Each completed question writes `procedureSteps` and `cellErrors` with explicit `misconception_tags`.
   - The Triage Engine sums mark deductions grouped by `misconception_tag`:
     ```python
     triage_summary = {
         "total_lost": 48,
         "breakdown": [
             {"tag": "net_vs_gross_confusion", "marks_lost": 16, "frequency": 4},
             {"tag": "sign_error_distribution", "marks_lost": 10, "frequency": 3},
             {"tag": "omitted_balance_b_d", "marks_lost": 6, "frequency": 2}
         ]
     }
     ```

2. **The 3-Step Cognitive Repair Sequence (`PostExamTriageModal.jsx`):**
   For the highest-loss misconception tag, the learner is guided through three immediate micro-steps:

   - **Step 1: Cognitive Conflict Probe**
     A single 1-tap multiple-choice question specifically designed to confront the false intuition.
     *Example:* "When a client pays R115 for a goods item that includes 15% VAT, how much VAT does the business owe SARS?"
     Options: `R15` (Bait) vs `R15 ÷ 1.15 = R13.04` (Bait) vs `R115 × (15/115) = R15` vs `15% of R100 = R15`.
   
   - **Step 2: SimuLearn Micro-Replay**
     Launches `SimuLearnPlayer.jsx` directly at the inflection keyframe where the specific calculation is performed, without forcing the student to watch the entire question from step 0.

   - **Step 3: Isomorphic Reattempt Pair**
     Calls the question generator with a fresh seed and the exact same subskill parameters to verify that the schema has been repaired:
     `generate_question(seed=fresh_seed, mode="elementary_vat_calc")`

## Audit Trail Persistence
All triage recommendations must be recorded both in the Firestore compute-ready document (`session_index/{sessionId}`) and in the PDF report generated by `report_generator.py`.
