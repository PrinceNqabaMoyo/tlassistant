- **Student Inputted Answer (canonical):** `153`
- **Expected / Memo Answer:** `153`
- **Official Worked Solution:** \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times b \times h
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 18 \times 17
\text{Area} = 153 \text{ cm}^2
- **Marking Evaluation:** **2 / 2** (✅ Correct)
