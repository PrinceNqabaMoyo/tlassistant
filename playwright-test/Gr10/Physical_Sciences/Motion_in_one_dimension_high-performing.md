> 
> 1. State the **Law of Conservation of Mechanical Energy**.
> 2. Calculate the gravitational potential energy ($E_p$) of the trolley at the top of the track.
> 3. State the kinetic energy ($E_k$) of the trolley at the moment of release.

   $490 = \frac{1}{2} (5) v^2$
   $v^2 = \frac{2 \times 490}{5} = 196.0$
   $v = \sqrt{196.0} = 14\text{ m}\cdot\text{s}^{-1}$
