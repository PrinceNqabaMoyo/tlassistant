> During the initial phase, oxygen delivery to the skeletal muscle cells is sufficient, but during peak exertion, an oxygen deficit develops.
> 
> 1. Name the three sequential stages of aerobic cellular respiration and state where each takes place in the cell.
> 2. Write down the chemical equation for aerobic cellular respiration.
> 3. Explain what happens in the athlete's muscle cells when oxygen becomes depleted (anaerobic respiration) and name the metabolic byproduct that accumulates.

- **Student Inputted Answer (canonical):** `1. Glycolysis (cytoplasm), Krebs cycle (mitochondrial matrix), Oxidative phosphorylation (mitochondrial cristae); 2. ...`
- **Expected / Memo Answer:** `1. Glycolysis (cytoplasm), Krebs cycle (mitochondrial matrix), Oxidative phosphorylation (mitochondrial cristae); 2. C6H12O6 + 6O2 -> 6CO2 +...`
- **Official Worked Solution:** 1. Stages of aerobic respiration [3]:
   - Glycolysis: Cytoplasm / Cytosol
   - Krebs cycle (Citric acid cycle): Mitochondrial matrix
   - Oxidative phosphorylation: Inner mitochondrial membrane / Cristae
2. Chemical equation: $\text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 + 6\text{O}_2 \xrightarrow{} 6\text{CO}_2 + 6\text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{ATP}$ [3]
3. Under oxygen deficit, anaerobic respiration (lactic acid fermentation) takes place [1]. Pyruvate is reduced to lactic acid [1], which accumulates and leads to muscle fatigue/cramping [1].
