> 1. Name the hormone responsible for maintaining water balance in the human body and state which gland secretes it.

- **Student Inputted Answer (canonical):** `1. ADH (Antidiuretic Hormone) secreted by the pituitary gland; 2. Osmoreceptors detect low water, pituitary releases ...`

- **Official Worked Solution:** 1. Antidiuretic Hormone (ADH) / Vasopressin [1], secreted by the posterior pituitary gland (hypophysis) [1].

   - ADH increases the water permeability of the distal convoluted tubules and collecting ducts of the kidney nephrons [1].
