> In a photoelectric experiment, ultraviolet radiation of frequency **$8.00e+14\text{ Hz}$** is shone onto the clean surface of a **Cesium (Cs)** cathode.
> The work function of Cesium (Cs) is **$3.42e-19\text{ J}$**.
> 
> 1. Define the term *work function* ($W_0$) of a metal.
> 2. Calculate the threshold frequency ($f_0$) of Cesium (Cs).
> 3. Calculate the maximum kinetic energy ($E_{k,\text{max}}$) of the ejected photoelectrons.
> 4. Calculate the maximum speed ($v_{\text{max}}$) with which photoelectrons leave the cathode surface.

   $5.30e-19 = 3.42e-19 + E_{k,\text{max}}$
   $E_{k,\text{max}} = 5.30e-19 - 3.42e-19 = 1.88e-19\text{ J}$

   $v_{\text{max}}^2 = \frac{2 \times 1.88e-19}{9{,}11 \times 10^{-31}} = 4.13e+11$
   $v_{\text{max}} = 6.43e+05\text{ m}\cdot\text{s}^{-1}$
