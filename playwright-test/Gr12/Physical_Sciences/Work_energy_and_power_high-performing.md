> 1. State the work-energy theorem in words.
> 2. Draw a labeled free-body diagram of all forces acting on the crate.

2. Free-body diagram [4]:
   - Normal force $F_N$ acting perpendicular upwards [1]
   - Gravitational force $F_g$ / $w$ acting vertically downwards [1]
   - Applied force $F_A$ directed at $0^\circ$ above horizontal [1]
   - Kinetic friction $f_k$ directed opposite to motion [1]
