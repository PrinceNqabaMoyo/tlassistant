5. Monthly PAYE [2]:
   $$\text{Monthly PAYE} = \frac{\text{R}83 261,68}{12} = \text{R}6 938,47$$ [M+A]
6. Progressive tax clarification [2]: The statement is FALSE. In South Africa's progressive tax system, only the portion of income exceeding the bracket threshold is taxed at the higher marginal percentage.
