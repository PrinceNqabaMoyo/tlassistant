> 
> 1. Classify each of the following supplies as **Standard-rated (15%)**, **Zero-rated (0%)**, or **Exempt**:
>    (a) Sale of Fresh fruit and vegetables.
>    (b) Long-term payment for Long-term residential rental accommodation.
>    (c) Purchase of Cleaning detergents.
> 
> 2. Explain the fundamental economic difference between a **Zero-rated supply** and an **Exempt supply** from the perspective of claiming Input VAT.
> 
> 3. A partner at Apex Wholesale Partnerships purchased a luxury home entertainment system for their private residence and instructed the accounts clerk to enter the invoice into the CPJ and claim Input VAT. - **Expected / Memo Answer:** `Zero-rated: Fresh fruit and vegetables; Exempt: Long-term residential rental accommodation; Standard: Cleaning detergents`
- **Official Worked Solution:** 1. Classification:
   (a) Fresh fruit and vegetables: Zero-rated (0% VAT)
   (b) Long-term residential rental accommodation: Exempt (No VAT charged)
   (c) Cleaning detergents: Standard-rated (15% VAT)

2. Economic difference:
   - On Zero-rated supplies, VAT is charged at 0%, but the registered vendor CAN claim back Input VAT paid on goods/services used to produce them.
   - On Exempt supplies, no VAT is charged, and the vendor CANNOT claim any Input VAT incurred in providing the exempt service.

3. Ethics & SARS consequences:
   - It is fraudulent/tax evasion to claim Input VAT on private personal expenditure because Input VAT is strictly deductible only for expenses incurred in the course of making taxable business supplies.
   - Consequences: SARS penalties (up to 200% understatement penalty), interest on overdue tax, and possible criminal prosecution.
