Privacy Statement & POPIA Minor Protection

Fundile is an educational teaching and learning platform built for South African Grades 7–12. We take personal data governance and learner protection with the utmost gravity. This statement sets out how personal information is collected, processed, and safeguarded in strict alignment with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), with special emphasis on Section 35 (General Prohibition on Processing of Personal Information Concerning Children).

🛡️ Section 35 Child Information Guarantee

Because Fundile serves minor learners (under 18 years of age), we operate under heightened statutory duty. We maintain a strict zero-advertising policy, zero data monetization, and zero sale or licensing of student responses to third parties or commercial AI LLM training datasets. Student data is processed exclusively for authentic educational assessment and curriculum mastery reporting.

1. What Information Fundile Collects

We only collect the minimum personal information strictly necessary to provide curriculum-aligned practice and teacher/school assessment moderation:

  • Account Identity: Learner or educator name, email address, school affiliation, and grade level.
  • Academic Telemetry: Stepwise answer inputs, scores, procedure attempt sequences, and diagnostic error tags (e.g. VAT net vs. gross confusion).
  • Institutional Metadata: Class codes, teacher-assigned tasks, and official school billing contact details.

2. What Fundile NEVER Collects

Unlike commercial social media or consumer software, Fundile enforces radical data minimization:

  • NO biometric or facial recognition data.
  • NO precise real-time geolocation or GPS tracking.
  • NO advertising identifiers or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • NO access to personal mobile photo galleries, contacts, or microphones.

3. Purpose of Processing (Educational Benefit)

Under Section 35(1)(a) of POPIA, learner data is processed with the consent of a competent person (parent/guardian or authorized school administrator) solely for:

  • Generating deterministic formative feedback and 3-tier pre-calculated hints.
  • Compiling printable PDF diagnostic progress reports for parents and teachers.
  • Assisting HODs and subject heads with mid-term pacing and curriculum moderation.

4. Zero LLM Training on Student Work

Fundile's core calculations (accounting ledgers, algebraic derivations, and physics formulas) are 100% deterministic code written in Python and SymPy. Student answer attempts and diagnostic logs are never fed into public foundational AI models (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or external providers) for model training or commercial ingestion.

5. Security, Encryption & Retention

All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption. Diagnostic data at rest is stored in enterprise-grade secured infrastructure with granular role-based access control (RBAC). Student submissions are retained only as long as necessary for the active academic cycle, after which inactive cohorts can be archived or permanently purged.

6. Parental & SGB Rights (Access & Erasure)

Under POPIA, parents, legal guardians, and school governing bodies have full rights to:

  • Request a copy of all diagnostic records held for their learner.
  • Request immediate correction of any inaccurate student records.
  • Request complete deletion ("right to be forgotten") upon leaving the institution.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@fundile.com.
